Privacy policy

We collect what we need to run a booking and nothing else. We do not sell data, and we never see your card number.

01What we collect

When you register a player we ask for the player’s name, date of birth and grade, and a parent or guardian’s name, email address and mobile number. You can add a second contact and a note for the coach.

  • Player name, date of birth and grade
  • Parent or guardian name, email and mobile
  • An optional second contact
  • Waiver and photo-release choices
  • Which programs you booked and when

02What we do not collect

We do not ask for your postal address, because the only thing it was ever used for was card billing and the payment page handles that itself.

We never see or store card numbers. Payment happens on Clover’s own hosted checkout, and what comes back to us is a confirmation that the payment succeeded.

03Why we hold it

To run the booking, to know who is in the building on a given day, to reach a parent if something happens during a session, and to email confirmations, reminders and receipts.

The date of birth is there to put a player in the right age group. It is not used for anything else.

04Who we share it with

Only the services that make the booking work.

  • Clover, which processes the payment
  • Constant Contact, which sends confirmations, reminders and receipts
  • Our website and database host

05Marketing email

Confirmations, reminders and receipts are part of the booking and are sent whether or not you join a mailing list.

Anything else is opt-in and every message has an unsubscribe link that works.

06How long we keep it

Registration and waiver records are kept for as long as the academy needs them for insurance and safeguarding, then deleted.

You can ask us to delete your details at any time by emailing info@dichiarobaseball.com. If a waiver is still in force for the season we will tell you what we have to keep and why.

Needs legal review

Retention periods and the rights section should be checked against New Jersey requirements and the academy’s insurance policy before this page is published.

07Children’s data

Most of the people who train here are under eighteen. Accounts and bookings are made by a parent or guardian, never by the player, and we do not market to children.

08Cookies

The site uses the small number of cookies needed to keep a booking together between pages. If analytics are added later, they will be named here and you will be asked first.

09Contacting us

Email info@dichiarobaseball.com or call (201) 773-6858. The academy is at 18-01 Pollitt Drive, Fair Lawn, NJ 07410.

Questions about any of this?

Call the facility on (201) 773-6858 or email info@dichiarobaseball.com and we will talk it through.
