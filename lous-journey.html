The record

What he did before he taught it.

Two of these numbers still stand in the Seton Hall books more than forty years later.

1980-83
Seton Hall University. Four-year scholarship player, four-year starter, captain in 1983.
Career walks
112. Tenth all time at Seton Hall.
Stolen bases
86. Seventh all time at Seton Hall.
1982
Cape Cod League, Yarmouth-Dennis Red Sox. Batted .321 against the best college pitching in the country.
1983
Drafted in the 19th round by the Baltimore Orioles. Signed and played Single A with the Newark Orioles.
2008-13
Assistant varsity coach, Don Bosco Prep. 2008 National High School Champions.
2021 onward
Assistant varsity coach, Old Tappan High School.
2000 onward
Teaching hitting and fielding, baseball and softball, every year since.

Why the academy exists

He built the room he wished he had.

A team practice has twenty players and forty-five minutes. Lou opened the academy in 2000 to teach the way he was taught in the cage: one player at a time, with enough reps to change something.

“Lou is the go-to baseball instructor in Bergen County. His knowledge, experience, and respect for the game are unmatched.”
Mike Sheppard Jr., Seton Hall Prep
