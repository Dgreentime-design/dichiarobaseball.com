Our story

We are baseball people.

Lou played four years at Seton Hall, hit .321 in the Cape Cod League and was drafted by the Orioles in 1983. He started teaching in 2000 and hasn’t stopped.

The academy is 5,500 square feet in Fair Lawn, staffed by coaches who played in high school, college and the pros. Groups stay small, so every player gets the reps and the coach sees every swing.

The facility

5,500 square feet, and the weather never matters.

On Pollitt Drive in Fair Lawn, ten minutes from Routes 208 and 4.

Inside the room

  • Batting cages for live pitching and machine work
  • Turf area for infield and fielding work
  • HitTrax simulator with full ball-flight data
  • Space for team practice and specialty camps
  • Hourly rentals for teams and individual players
