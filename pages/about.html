Lou DiChiaro has been teaching hitting and fielding since 2000.

Our story

We are baseball people.

Lou DiChiaro played four years at Seton Hall, spent a summer in the Cape Cod League, and was drafted by the Baltimore Orioles in 1983. He started teaching in 2000, and he has been at it every season since. His full journey is here.

The academy grew out of that work. It is a 5,500 square foot room in Fair Lawn with cages, turf and HitTrax, and a staff who all played at college or professional level. Nobody here is reading a drill out of a binder.

More than two thousand athletes have trained here, from eight-year-olds learning to hold a bat properly to high school players chasing a college roster spot. The camps and clinics are capped on purpose. Small groups mean more swings, more ground balls, and a coach who can actually see what a player is doing wrong.

The goal has not changed. Every player leaves better than they came in, and every player enjoys the work. That is the whole business.

How we teach

Four things we will not compromise on.

01

Small groups, real reps

Camps and clinics are capped on purpose. More swings, more ground balls, and a coach who can see what one player is doing wrong.

02

Taught by people who played

Every instructor played at college or professional level. Three were drafted by major league clubs.

03

Measured, not guessed

HitTrax gives exit velocity, launch angle and spray charts. Progress is a number a player can see, not an opinion they have to take on faith.

04

A positive experience first

Players who enjoy the work keep showing up. Confidence and discipline come from the same place, and both take time.

The facility

5,500 square feet, and the weather never matters.

The academy sits on Pollitt Drive in Fair Lawn, ten minutes from Route 208 and Route 4. It is open year round, so a January lesson looks exactly like a June one.

Inside the room

  • Batting cages for live pitching and machine work
  • Turf area for infield and fielding work
  • HitTrax simulator with full ball-flight data
  • Space for team practice and specialty camps
  • Hourly rentals for teams and individual players
