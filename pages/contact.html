Before you write

The four things people ask us most.

How do I sign up for a camp?

Everything is online. Pick the program, add the player, sign the waiver and pay. It takes about three minutes and you get the dates by email straight away.

Do we have to sign a waiver?

Yes, once per player per season. It is built into the sign-up, so there is nothing to print, sign and post back.

What ages do you take?

Six to eighteen across the programs. If you are not sure which one fits, tell us the age and what they play and we will point you at the right one.

Can I book with a specific coach?

Yes. Name them in your message and we will check their availability. Softball pitchers, ask for Diana or Sam.
