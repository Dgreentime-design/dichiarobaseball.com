How booking works

Tell us what you need

A cage for half an hour, the full floor for a team, or a HitTrax session. Message us or call (201) 773-6858.

02

Rentals run around camps and lessons, so we check the schedule and come back with what is open that week.

03

Pay online when you book, or settle at the desk. Bring your own bats and helmets, or use ours.
