# Testing the new site

Michael, this is a private test copy of the new dichiarobaseball.com:

**https://dichiarobaseballcom-rgr6-git-review-box-to-box-product-design.vercel.app**

It works like the real site, but nothing you do here is real. No card is
charged, nothing is published, and search engines are told to ignore it.
Registrations and messages you send land in a separate test table, not with
real families. Try it on your phone first, then on a computer if you can.

## Five things to try, in order

1. **Register for a camp and pay online.** From the homepage, open the
   Little League Training Camp, press Register, add a made-up player, sign
   the waiver, choose "Online now" and pay. You should end on a green
   "is registered" screen with a reference number.
2. **Do it again, paying at the facility, then by check.** Same steps,
   choosing "At the facility" or "By check" on the last step. Nothing is
   charged either way.
3. **Send a message** from the Contact page, and a team enquiry from the
   Team & town camps page. Each ends with a reference starting EQ-.
4. **Ask about HitTrax.** On the homepage, press "Request a HitTrax
   session" and send the message. The form should already say "Cage or
   facility rental".
5. **Add a camp to your calendar.** On any camp page, press "Add to
   calendar" and choose Apple Calendar or Google Calendar.

## What "paying" looks like here

When you choose Online now, the test copy shows a plain page that says
**"Mock checkout. Not a real payment. No card is taken."** Press
**Approve** to act as a successful payment, or **Decline** to see what a
family sees when a card fails. You never enter card details on this copy.
On the live site this step is Clover's secure payment page.

## Sending feedback

For anything that looks wrong or confuses you, send Daniel:

- **The page** (its address, or its name, such as "Infield camp page").
- **What you expected** to happen.
- **What happened** instead.
- **A screenshot** if you can.

One message with a list is perfect. Small things count: a wrong date, a
price, a word that reads oddly.
