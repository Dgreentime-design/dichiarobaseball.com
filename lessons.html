One flag on the approved pricing

These are the current prices and they stand. One thing to look at when there is time: five private hitting lessons at $430 is $86 each, and ten at $820 is $82 each, against a single thirty-minute lesson at $75. A parent doing the arithmetic reads that as paying more to buy in bulk, which is the opposite of what a package is for.

It resolves itself if the packages are forty-five minute lessons, priced against the $90 rate, in which case the page should say so. The pitching packages do not have this problem.

Good to know

Before you book.

Waiver first

Every player signs a waiver once per season before their first lesson. It is part of the online booking, so there is nothing to print.

Packages are not refundable

Once a five or ten pack is bought it is not refunded. It does not expire during the season, and any instructor on staff can teach the sessions.

Bring what you have

Own bat, own glove, own helmet if you have them. If you do not, we have equipment here and it costs nothing extra.

Parents can watch

There is a spot to sit and watch. We ask that you let the coach do the coaching during the session and save the notes for the drive home.

Start with one lesson.

Thirty minutes tells you plenty about whether this is the right fit. Book a single lesson first and buy a package later if it works.
